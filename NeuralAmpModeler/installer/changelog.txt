NAM On Steroids 3.0.0

- Redesigned editor with a draggable signal chain and a dedicated block inspector.
- Add, remove, reorder, and bypass Gate, NAM, and Cab blocks.
- Route a gate's detector from its own input, the chain input, or the output of any preceding block.
- Load HyperA2, HyperWaveNet, ConcatWaveNet, and ConcatLSTM with their own controls.
  Parametric models process at their native model sample rate.
- The original tone stack remains available for traditional NAM files.
- Host sessions and internal presets store the signal chain, sidechain choices, and model control values.
